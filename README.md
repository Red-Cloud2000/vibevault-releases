# vibevault-releases
Downloads for VibeVault: the Windows desktop workspace for vibe coding. Get the latest installer from Releases; the app keeps itself up to date.

VibeVault keeps every AI assistant, dev tool and project one click away, and shows exactly what your AI subscriptions cost, month by month. Everything stays on your computer.

## Install

1. Open **[Releases](../../releases/latest)** and download `VibeVault-Setup-<version>.exe`.
2. Run it. VibeVault installs for your Windows user only, with no administrator rights.
3. If Windows shows **"Windows protected your PC"**, click **More info** › **Run anyway**. The installer isn't code-signed yet; this warning is expected.

**Requirements:** Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit).

## Updates

VibeVault checks for new versions on its own. When one is ready, a card appears at the bottom of the sidebar: click **Download**, then **Restart to install**. You can also use **Help › Check for Updates**.

Each update is verified against its published SHA-512 checksum before it is installed.

## What's inside

- 🚀 **Launch everything**: your apps and tools as cards, with links per environment and one-click launch profiles
- 💳 **Track AI subscriptions**: renewals, price history, reminders and pay-as-you-go charges
- 📈 **See where money goes**: trends, a breakdown per subscription and a 12-month forecast, in EGP at each payment day's rate
- ☁️ **Encrypted Google Drive backups**: encrypted on your computer with a recovery key only you hold
- 🔐 **Private by design**: no account, no tracking, no ads; optional app lock
